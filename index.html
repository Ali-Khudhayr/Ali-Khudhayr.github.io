01 · About

Power engineer in the making

I am a final-year Electrical Engineering student specializing in Power & Machines at King Abdulaziz University, holding a 4.68 / 5.00 GPA and a Certificate of Academic Excellence.

My work spans power-flow and fault analysis in PowerWorld and Simulink, synchronous-machine testing, PIC18 and ESP32 embedded systems, and data-driven calibration of an autonomous vehicle. My senior design project builds a smart home energy-management system with priority-based load control.

Outside the lab I have supervised student teams, built data reports for a multi-city retail operation, taught as a university TA, taught weekly in a mosque memorization circle since 2024, and memorized 20 juz of the Qur’an through six certified levels. I also produced a 29-video animated series that explains six power-engineering courses.
